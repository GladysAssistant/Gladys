# Moon image credit

`moon.png` is a crop of the lunar disk from the "Moon Phase and Libration, 2025"
visualization by NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

- Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5415
- Visualizer: Ernie Wright (USRA)
- Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio
- DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17145129

NASA imagery is in the public domain and free to reuse. The image was cropped
to the lunar disk, resized to 256x256, masked to a circle with a transparent
background and quantized to reduce its size.
